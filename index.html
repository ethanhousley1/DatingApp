Philosophy

Mystery isn't a bug in meeting someone. It's most of the fun.

Part of why meeting someone new is exciting is that they're new, funny in a way you can't predict yet, and a little mysterious. A profile deletes all three before a single word is exchanged. We just give the mystery back — on purpose, and only for as long as it takes to earn your way past it.

How the reveal works

Nothing is unlocked until you both mean it.

It's mutual, every time. Neither of you can unlock the other alone — the door only opens from both sides at once.
