Why it exists

The people still looking are the ones getting buried.

When the next profile is always one swipe away, no conversation is worth saving. Part of why meeting someone is exciting is that they’re new, funny in a way you can’t predict, and a little mysterious. Three at a time gives each person room to be that, and leaves more of them to find out as you talk.
