Why it exists

The people still looking are the ones getting buried.

Part of why meeting someone is exciting is that they’re new, funny in a way you can’t predict, and a little mysterious. A profile deletes all three before a word is exchanged. We give that back on purpose — and only for as long as it takes to earn your way past it.
