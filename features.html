04 · Icebreakers

For when you don't know how to start.

A way into a conversation — not decoration on a profile someone reads before deciding you're worth talking to.

“What's something you believed for way too long before someone corrected you?”
“Tell me about a trip that didn't go as planned.”
“What's a small thing that's disproportionately made your week better?”
“What would your friends say you're weirdly competitive about?”

07 · Your quality score

A measure of how you talk, not how you look.

Asking real questions and engaging in good faith raises it. It quietly unlocks more people per week, faster reveals, occasional early fragments, and pairing with other high-quality conversationalists. It's personal — never a public rank, never a number anyone else sees.
